Percentiles & Ordered Data Exploration Tool

Generate or upload discrete or continuous datasets, inspect ordered values, hover or focus over data points to reveal percentiles, and calculate values or percentile ranks interactively.

1. Data Selection & Generation

Separate numbers with commas, spaces, or new lines.
Select a plain text (.txt) or comma-separated (.csv) data file.

2. Interactive Percentile Queries

Enter a percentile between 0 and 100 (e.g., 75 for 75th percentile / Q3).
Enter any value (contained in dataset or custom) to find its percentile rank.

3. Ordered Dataset & Hover Percentile Inspector

Hover or Focus Inspector: Move your cursor over any value in the grid below to inspect its rank and exact percentile.
Data values sorted from smallest to largest ($n = 0$). Use cursor or Tab key to navigate values.
